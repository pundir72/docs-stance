# Clinical Summary Improvement Plan

A proposed V2 summary workflow is ready for clinical and technical review. It includes central scoring rules for Safety, Severity, Urgency, Criticality, and risk bands; safer VALD handling using valid trial averages and bilateral asymmetry calculation; protection against treating missing or zero readings as patient deficits; clearer separation between patient concerns, missing information, and programme recommendations; and checks to detect if important concern scores or numbers change between the detailed report and dashboard summary. These are proposed improvements, not approved production changes. The `new-summary-dev` collection is available for safe development testing and comparison without changing production summaries in `new-summary`.

Our proposed implementation is to simplify and tighten the AI prompt so every Area of Concern must be based on the latest relevant patient note or VALD session, show its source date and evidence, and cannot be created for missing tests, programme gaps, or unsupported assumptions. The backend will calculate the final score from Safety, Severity, and Urgency using one approved formula instead of trusting an AI-generated total; validate that score components follow agreed clinical limits; recalculate VALD asymmetry from valid left/right trial averages; reject null, zero, or one-sided readings as asymmetry; and prevent trend claims when only one session exists. We will also make the concise dashboard summary preserve the same approved concern, score, evidence, and measurements as the detailed report; block any incomplete, inconsistent, or unsupported result from being saved; record why a result was blocked for review; and test these rules against representative patient cases in `new-summary-dev`. We need doctor and client approval of these proposed rules before implementation, then we will build, test, compare results with current summaries, and present the output for final clinical approval before production release.
