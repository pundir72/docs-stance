# Clinician Agent — Overview

## What is the Clinician Agent?

The Clinician Agent is an AI-powered assistant that helps clinicians complete patient assessment forms more quickly.

Instead of typing every detail manually, a clinician can speak or enter notes in everyday language. The system understands the information and places it into the appropriate sections of the clinical form.

## How does it work?

1. The clinician opens a patient assessment form.
2. The clinician speaks or types their clinical notes.
3. If voice is used, the system converts the recording into text.
4. The AI identifies information such as exercises, sets, repetitions, assessment results, patient feedback, and exertion level.
5. The relevant fields in the form are automatically updated.
6. The system can also provide suggestions and real-time recommendations.
7. The clinician reviews the result and makes any necessary corrections before saving it.

## Simple example

A clinician might say:

> Add squats for three sets of ten repetitions and record the patient's exertion level as six.

The Clinician Agent can place this information into the exercise plan and RPE sections of the form automatically.

## What information can it help capture?

- Exercise plans
- Sets, repetitions, load, and duration
- Clinical advice and comments
- Objective assessment results
- Patient-reported symptoms and feedback
- Rate of Perceived Exertion (RPE)
- Suggested next steps and recommendations

## Key benefits

- Reduces time spent typing and completing forms
- Allows clinicians to document information naturally
- Produces structured information that is easier to review
- Helps reduce repetitive administrative work
- Supports more consistent clinical documentation
- Keeps the clinician in control of the final record

## Where is information stored?

The system temporarily stores session information so that it can maintain context, avoid unnecessary repeated processing, and support a smoother user experience.

The exact storage and retention approach can be configured according to the organisation's privacy, security, and compliance requirements.

## Important note

The Clinician Agent is an assistant, not a replacement for professional clinical judgement. All generated form entries, suggestions, and recommendations should be reviewed and approved by a qualified clinician before they become part of the patient's final record.

## In one sentence

The Clinician Agent turns a clinician's spoken or typed notes into an organised clinical form, helping reduce documentation time while leaving final decisions with the clinician.
